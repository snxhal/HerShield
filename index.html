Constitutional & Legal Cyber Rights for Women in India

Know your protections under Indian law regarding digital harassment, privacy, and online safety.

Right to Anonymity & Privacy

Section 72 & 72A (IT Act)

Breach of confidentiality and privacy. If any person secures access to electronic records or personal data without consent and discloses it, they face imprisonment up to 3 years.

Protection Against Stalking

Section 354D (IPC / Equivalent BNS)

Makes cyberstalking a punishable criminal offense. Monitoring a woman's online browsing, social media activity, or repeatedly contacting her despite clear disinterest is illegal.

Obscene & Threatening Messages

Section 67 & 67A (IT Act)

Publishing or transmitting obscene material, sexually explicit content, or morphing photos online carries strict jail terms up to 5 to 7 years with heavy fines.

Right to Zero FIR

Supreme Court Guideline

A woman can file a cyber crime complaint at **any police station** across India, regardless of where the cyber incident actually took place. The police cannot refuse registration.
